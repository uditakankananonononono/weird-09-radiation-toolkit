That is a mature conclusion.

(2) Biggest remaining scientific hole
After the phylogenetic issue is honestly bounded, the largest remaining weakness becomes:
The candidates lack functional discrimination.
The paper now has a strong computational discovery pipeline:
orthogroup enrichment,
stress-matched controls,
domain confirmation,
phylogenetic identifiability analysis.
But the surviving candidates remain a ranked list of hypotheses.
The problem is visible in the wording:
“DUF4385 protein --- the last representing genuinely novel candidate space”
and:
“PAD-porphyrin binding protein”
These are interesting, but a judge will ask:
“Which candidate is most likely to explain radiation resistance, and what evidence separates it from a passenger protein?”
The project currently demonstrates:
associated with radiation-resistant lineages
but not:
mechanistically involved in radiation resistance
That distinction is the remaining gap.
(3) One laptop-feasible addition that would most improve the project
Add a candidate prioritization model based on independent evidence layers.
Not another enrichment test.
The project already has enough statistics.
The next useful step is to build a transparent evidence score for each candidate using features such as:
recurrence across resistant lineages,
absence from stress-only controls,
domain novelty,
predicted localization,
conservation pattern,
gene neighborhood conservation (especially in bacterial candidates),
structural similarity to known DNA repair/oxidative-defense proteins.
Then predefine:
“Candidate priority is determined by evidence integration, not p-value alone.”
For example:
A DUF4385 candidate might rank highly because:
rare in controls,
recurrent in resistant taxa,
conserved architecture,
no obvious housekeeping function.
A peroxiredoxin might rank lower because:
biologically relevant,
but ubiquitous stress protein.
This would transform the project from:
“Here are ten candidates.”
into:
“Here is a principled computational framework for prioritizing radiation-resistance mechanisms.”
This is feasible on a laptop.
