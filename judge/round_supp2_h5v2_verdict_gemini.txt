Protocol Amendment v2: Prioritization Metric & Reference Set Benchmark
1. Objective & Deliverable

The pipeline deliverable is defined as a ranked candidate list accompanied by a two-tier evidence classification matrix for each candidate:

Tier 2 (Mechanism-Level Evidence): Biochemical/domain-architecture mechanism consistent with repair or protection against classes C1–C5 damage, without direct family-level literature binding.
