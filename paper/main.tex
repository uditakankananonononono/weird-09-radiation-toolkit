\section{Introduction}
Comparative genomics is full of convergence claims that later collapsed under scrutiny: candidates that were pipeline artifacts, annotation echoes, or within-family similarities mistaken for cross-origin signal. This paper takes the collapse risk as its object. We build a pre-registered screening framework whose explicit job is to \emph{prevent} a false convergence claim - locked gates before outcome inspection, permutation nulls rather than asymptotic ones where asymptotics are structurally unattainable, an identifiability score that states what the panel can and cannot decide before it decides anything, baseline selection rules the framework must beat to earn its complexity, and power analyses that attach a detectable-effect size to every null - and we run it on a question where an affirmative answer would have been headline-worthy: whether the three independent origins of extreme radiation resistance, in Deinococcaceae, Tardigrada, and Bdelloidea, converged on the same oxidative-defense molecular machinery.
The molecular answer, under the framework's own locked gates, is largely no - and the framework is what makes that \emph{no} worth publishing. At every stage where a looser pipeline would have printed a candidate list, a locked check either falsified the candidate, showed the test was structurally incapable of detecting the claimed effect, or showed a trivial baseline did as well. The nulls below are therefore not the absence of a result: each is a quantified statement (with seeds, power limits, and archived buggy outputs) about what convergence evidence this class of panel can support. A screen that cannot say no convincingly cannot say yes convincingly either; this one can, and does.

\section{Methods (pre-registered; amendments dated 2026-09-26)}
Panel: 5 resistant proteomes available of the 6 locked (Deinococcus radiodurans, D. geothermalis, D. deserti [origin 1: Deinococcaceae]; Ramazzottius varieornatus [origin 2: Tardigrada]; Adineta vaga [origin 3: Bdelloidea]) vs 6 sensitive controls (Thermus aquaticus, T. thermophilus, Hypsibius exemplaris, Caenorhabditis elegans, Drosophila melanogaster, Escherichia coli). Milnesium tardigradum has no reference assembly; Tardigrada origin is single-proteome (power caveat carried throughout).

Orthogroup calling: MMseqs2 v17 sensitive clustering (min\_seq\_id 0.3, coverage 0.5, cov-mode 1), 154,665 proteins. Linclust used only as sensitivity; subset concordance (20k proteins): linclust pair purity 0.914 vs sensitive, retention 0.363, so all formal claims rest on sensitive clustering.

Formal test (locked 11:15 and 11:29 amendments, before outcome inspection): per orthogroup, one-sided Fisher exact on presence/absence (5 resistant vs 6 sensitive; the sixth locked resistant species, \emph{Milnesium tardigradum}, has no reference assembly) and one-sided Mann-Whitney U on zero-filled copy numbers. The BH-FDR gate was replaced pre-outcome by 1000 label permutations with per-orthogroup empirical $p$, after W03's power analysis showed BH-FDR is structurally impossible at this panel size (minimum achievable Fisher $p = 1/\binom{11}{5} = 2.16\times10^{-3}$ at the true 5-vs-6 panel vs BH rank-1 threshold $\approx 10^{-6}$). Gate: empirical $p<0.05$, resistant-direction, replication in $\geq 2$ of 3 independent resistant origins.

Candidate annotation: NCBI esummary + hmmscan (HMMER 3.3.2) vs Pfam-A (full-sequence $E<10^{-10}$).


\subsection{Formal framework}
Each orthogroup $g$ is a partition of the panel proteomes into member sets; for species $i$ let $n_{gi} \ge 0$ be the copy number and $x_{gi} = \mathbf{1}[n_{gi} > 0]$ the presence indicator. The resistant and sensitive label vectors $y \in \{0,1\}^{11}$ are fixed by the panel definition. The presence/absence arm applies a one-sided Fisher exact test to the $2\times2$ table of $x_g$ against $y$; under complete separation (all resistant present, all sensitive absent) the minimum achievable value is
\begin{equation}
p_{\min} \;=\; \frac{1}{\binom{11}{5}} \;=\; 2.16\times10^{-3},
\end{equation}
which motivated replacing the BH-FDR gate by permutation calibration (pre-outcome amendment). The empirical $p$-value for orthogroup $g$ over $B=1000$ label permutations $\pi_b$ is
\begin{equation}
\hat{p}_g \;=\; \frac{1 + \sum_{b=1}^{B} \mathbf{1}\!\left[ T(x_g, \pi_b(y)) \ge T(x_g, y) \right]}{1 + B},
\end{equation}
with statistic $T$ the resistant-direction enrichment score; the empirical-$p$ estimator floor is $1/(1+B) = 1/1001 \approx 10^{-3}$; the observed $\hat p = 0.013$ ($13/1001$) sits 13 permutation counts above that floor and is a genuine count-based estimate, not a floor-limited value. The copy-number arm uses a one-sided Mann-Whitney statistic
\begin{equation}
U_g \;=\; \sum_{i:\, y_i = 1} \sum_{j:\, y_j = 0} \mathbf{1}\!\left[ n_{gi} > n_{gj} \right] + \tfrac{1}{2}\,\mathbf{1}\!\left[ n_{gi} = n_{gj} \right]
\end{equation}
on zero-filled copy numbers. The convergence gate additionally requires replication across independent origins:
\begin{equation}
\sum_{k \in \{\text{Deino},\, \text{Tardi},\, \text{Bdello}\}} \mathbf{1}\!\left[ \max_{i \in k} x_{gi} = 1 \right] \;\ge\; 2.
\end{equation}

For the specificity layer, each v1 candidate was re-clustered with four stress-tolerant but radiation-sensitive controls and re-tested identically; a candidate keeps the label radiation-specific only if
\begin{equation}
\left| \{ c \in \text{stress controls} : x_{gc} = 1 \} \right| \;\le\; 1,
\end{equation}
otherwise it is re-labeled broad-stress adaptation. Domain-level confirmation searches each candidate's full Pfam architecture $\mathcal{D}_g = \{d_1, \dots, d_m\}$ against the five confirmation proteomes with family HMMs, accepting a proteome as supporting iff
\begin{equation}
\exists\, \text{protein } q : \bigwedge_{d \in \mathcal{D}_g} \left[ E(q, d) \le 10^{-5} \right],
\end{equation}
and confirming the candidate iff at least two of five proteomes support it. The phylogenetic layer (H4--H4c) maps presence onto a fixed cladogram $\tau$ and counts minimum gains by Fitch reconstruction,
\begin{equation}
N_{\text{ind}}(g) \;=\; \min_{\text{ASR}} \; \sum_{e \in \tau} \mathbf{1}\!\left[ 0 \to 1 \text{ on } e,\; e \text{ subtends a resistant leaf} \right],
\end{equation}
against a null of $10^{4}$ within-strata presence shuffles $\sigma_r$ preserving stratum counts,
\begin{equation}
p_{\text{lower}}(g) \;=\; \frac{1 + \sum_{r} \mathbf{1}\!\left[ N_{\text{ind}}^{\sigma_r} \le N_{\text{ind}}(g) \right]}{1 + 10^{4}},
\end{equation}
which proved degenerate on saturated strata (zero null variance), yielding the identifiability boundary reported in Section 3.4.

\subsection{Pipeline definition (end-to-end operator form; amendment 2026-09-27 12:08 IST)}
Collected, the framework is a single operator from raw inputs to a decision-relevant verdict. The inputs are the panel proteomes $\mathcal{P} = \{P_1, \dots, P_{20}\}$, the fixed resistant/sensitive label vector $y$, and the background orthogroup universe $\mathcal{M}$ ($|\mathcal{M}| = 52{,}786$). The pipeline is the composition
\begin{equation}
\Phi(\mathcal{P}, y, \mathcal{M}) \;=\; A \circ D \circ S \circ E \circ C(\mathcal{P}),
\end{equation}
where $C$ clusters proteomes into orthogroups; $E$ is the enrichment gate, $E(g) = \mathbf{1}[\hat{p}_g < \alpha_{\mathrm{emp}}] \wedge \mathbf{1}[\text{origins}(g) \ge 2]$ (empirical permutation $p$ and independent-origin replication, equations above); $S$ is the stress-control specificity filter; $D$ is the Pfam-architecture domain confirmation against independent proteomes; and $A$ is the phylogenetic identifiability audit of Section 4.5, which returns the class of equation (13) for every candidate, candidate or not. The output is \emph{not} a scalar score: each candidate receives the verdict tuple
\begin{equation}
v(g) \;=\; \bigl( \mathrm{label}(g),\; \mathrm{evidence\ tier}(g),\; \mathrm{class}(g) \bigr),
\end{equation}
mapped onto the ordered confidence scale
\begin{equation}
\begin{array}{@{}c@{}} \textsc{confirmed-convergent} \;>\; \textsc{candidate-testable} \;>\; \textsc{candidate-underpowered} \\[2pt] {} \;>\; \textsc{candidate-untestable} \;>\; \textsc{rejected}, \end{array}
\end{equation}
where the first tier requires $\mathrm{class}(g) = \textsc{testable}$ \emph{and} a significant convergence statistic, and a scalar collapse is deliberately refused: on any panel where the audit returns \textsc{impossible} or \textsc{underpowered}, a numeric ``confidence'' would manufacture precision the data cannot support. The identifiability class is therefore a \emph{precondition} for interpreting every other stage's output: enrichment, specificity, and domain confirmation establish that a candidate is real and radiation-associated; only the audit establishes whether the panel could ever have told us otherwise. This is the framework's stated purpose - a pipeline that prevents false convergence, including against itself: applied to its own output (Table 2), it returns 9 \textsc{impossible} and 1 \textsc{underpowered}, and no candidate is reported as convergent.

\subsection{Specificity layer on the extended panel (amendment 2026-09-26, 13:18 IST)}
The panel was extended pre-data with four stress-tolerant but radiation-\emph{sensitive} controls --- \emph{Pyrococcus furiosus}, \emph{Sulfolobus solfataricus}, \emph{Lactobacillus plantarum}, and \emph{Saccharomyces cerevisiae} --- chosen to separate radiation-specific retention from generic stress adaptation. All 168,186 proteins from the 15 proteomes were re-clustered de novo at the locked parameters (min.\ seq.\ id.\ 0.3, coverage 0.5, cov-mode 1), yielding 60,164 clusters. The locked gates were re-run identically on this v2 clustering, and each v1 candidate was then required to be absent from at least 3 of the 4 new controls to retain the label \emph{radiation-specific}; otherwise it is re-labeled \emph{broad-stress adaptation}.

All 12 v1 representative proteins mapped into v2 clusters. \textbf{10 of 12 candidates pass both the re-run enrichment gate (empirical $p=0.013$--$0.017$, 1000 permutations) and the specificity layer}, each with Deinococcaceae+Bdelloidea origins and at most one control-species member. Two honest negatives: (i) the v1 lead, the AhpC-TSA peroxiredoxin (UJR09360.1), is present in 3 of 4 stress-tolerant controls and is re-labeled broad-stress adaptation --- biologically consistent, since peroxiredoxins are near-universal stress proteins; (ii) the HPPK/pterin candidate (UJR10666.1) lost its Deinococcaceae members in the larger panel (Bdelloid-only cluster, empirical $p=0.449$) and no longer shows cross-kingdom convergence.

The oxidative-defense axis survives the specificity layer through the DSBA thiol-disulfide oxidoreductase and the flavin reductase candidates, now supported as radiation-specific rather than generic stress adaptations. The surviving set additionally includes the ankyrin scaffold, PAD-porphyrin binding protein, GNAT acetyltransferase, DHH/DHHA2 phosphoesterase, HAD hydrolase, GDP-mannose dehydrogenase, amidase, and the uncharacterized DUF4385 protein --- the last representing genuinely novel candidate space.


\subsection{Domain-level orthogonal confirmation (amendment 2026-09-26, 20:21 IST)}
As an independent check, the panel was extended with five radiation-resistant proteomes never used in the screen: \emph{Deinococcus radiophilus}, \emph{D.~proteolyticus}, \emph{D.~maricopensis}, \emph{Adineta ricciae}, and \emph{Rotaria magnacalcarata} (95,758 proteins; accessions and hashes in the ledger). For each radiation-specific candidate, its complete v1 Pfam domain set was searched against all five new proteomes (64 family HMMs; domain calls at $E\le 10^{-5}$, fixed before candidate-level results). \textbf{9 of 10 candidates are confirmed}: their full domain architectures recur in at least one protein of at least two of the five new proteomes (eight at 5/5 support, one at 4/5). The five-domain amidase architecture (UJR17759.1) found no complete match and is labeled ``domain-level support absent in extended panel'' without removal from the candidate set (locked rule); the HPPK candidate that failed v2 enrichment is likewise unconfirmed, a consistent picture.


\subsection{Phylogenetically controlled convergence test (amendments H4--H4c, 2026-09-26)}
The strongest remaining objection to the v2 labels is phylogenetic confounding: the three \emph{Deinococcus} proteomes (like the two \emph{Thermus} controls) are not independent replicates, so a label permutation that treats species as exchangeable only partially randomizes family-level structure. We therefore implemented a phylogenetically controlled convergence test, locked before computation (amendment H4): for each radiation-specific candidate, presence/absence was mapped onto a fixed 15-species cladogram (topology from NCBI taxonomy, unit branch lengths), and the minimum number of independent gains into resistant-subtending branches was computed by Fitch-parsimony ancestral-state reconstruction (the minimum-gain count is method-independent; a PastML maximum-likelihood cross-check is queued). The null was 10{,}000 structure-preserving permutations that shuffle presence assignments only within exchangeable strata (Deinococcaceae, Thermus, other controls), never across.
Formally, let $x_g \in \{0,1\}^{|\mathrm{leaves}(\tau)|}$ be the presence vector of candidate $g$ on the fixed cladogram $\tau$. The Fitch bottom-up pass assigns each internal node $v$ the intersection of its children's state sets when non-empty, else their union, and the minimum-gain statistic is the number of union events,
\begin{equation}
G(x_g;\tau) \;=\; \sum_{v \in \mathrm{int}(\tau)} \mathbf{1}\!\left[ S_v = S_{v_1} \cup S_{v_2} \right],
\end{equation}
where $S_v$ is the Fitch state set of node $v$ with children $v_1, v_2$, and a union event implies exactly one gain on the branch subtending the child absent the state. The structure-preserving null draws permutations $\pi_b$ that shuffle presence labels only within exchangeable strata $\mathcal{S} \in \{\text{Deinococcaceae}, \text{Thermus}, \text{other controls}\}$, and the corrected (lower-tail, amendment H4c) empirical convergence $p$-value is
\begin{equation}
\hat{p}^{\,\mathrm{conv}}_g \;=\; \frac{1 + \sum_{b=1}^{B} \mathbf{1}\!\left[ G(\pi_b(x_g);\tau) \le G(x_g;\tau) \right]}{1 + B},
\end{equation}
with $B = 10{,}000$; the lower tail is the convergence-consistent direction because scattered presence forces \emph{more} gains, so unusually \emph{few} gains reject the structured null.

\textbf{The test returned a structural null}: for all ten candidates the null distribution has zero variance (observed 2 gains, null median 2, $p=1.0$). The cause is identifiability, not biology: every candidate is present in all three Deinococcaceae, and the Tardigrada and Bdelloidea origins are singletons whose labels cannot be permuted, so no within-strata shuffle can move the signal. Resistance labels are perfectly confounded with tree topology on this panel.

We then locked an extended-panel arm (amendment H4b) adding the five confirmation lineages (three further Deinococcaceae, two further bdelloids; presence at the domain level only, per the confirmation-arm rule), and, after inspection revealed a tail-direction bug in our own locked statistic (scattered presence requires \emph{more} gains, so the convergence-consistent direction is the lower tail), corrected it under a new locked amendment (H4c) before re-evaluation. On the corrected test, 9 of 10 candidates remain degenerate --- saturated strata shuffle identically, so the null is constant --- and the single candidate with partially occupied strata (the five-domain amidase UJR17759.1; 3/6 Deinococcaceae, 1/3 bdelloids) shows 2 observed gains against a null median of 3 ($p=0.299$, not significant).

\subsection{Saturated co-retention arm (judge-redirected, amendment 2026-09-27 08:59 IST; hollow win reported)}
A judge redirect consult (round 4, Gemini) proposed reframing the finding as conserved co-retention rarity: statistic $S(g)$ = product of within-clade presence completeness over the three resistant clades, complete tier $S=1.0$ with zero sensitive copies, empirical $P$ against all $M=52{,}786$ background orthogroups, win at $P<0.005$ - locked before any $S$ value was inspected. The background configuration is indeed extreme: \textbf{zero} of 52{,}786 background orthogroups attain complete co-retention with control absence ($P=1.9\times10^{-5}$, the estimator floor, below the locked threshold). However, \textbf{zero of the 12 candidates attain the tier either} (all are 2-of-3-origin calls; Tardigrada is a one-species clade, so any candidate absent from \emph{R.\ varieornatus} scores $S=0$). The mechanical win is therefore hollow: the rarity statement is true but describes a configuration no candidate reaches, and the reframed claim is \textbf{not earned}. We report this rather than relax $S$ post hoc; the identifiability boundary above remains the terminal statement, and the consult's two data-dependent directions (a RELAX/codeml selection-intensity test and a dispersed-origin panel expansion) are staged with their own locked amendments.

\textbf{Identifiability boundary (quantified; redirect in progress).} On a panel whose resistant lineages form saturated clades, the structured-presence-permutation test implemented here cannot separate convergence from clade structure; power exists only for phylogenetically dispersed or partially occupied strata. The strengthened claim ``convergence beyond what species relatedness predicts'' is therefore \emph{not} earned by any candidate at this stage, and none receives it. All ten candidates retain their v2 radiation-specific labels with an explicit phylogenetic-structure caveat (locked rule: the confirmation layer never removes candidates). This boundary is not accepted as terminal: a redirection is in progress on three axes - (i) test designs that escape the saturation degeneracy on the same data (gene-tree/species-tree reconciliation, selection-regime comparisons on candidate orthogroups, phylogenetic regression of resistance on presence); (ii) additional phylogenetically dispersed resistant genomes that make the structured null non-degenerate; (iii) reframings in which clade-saturating cross-kingdom presence itself becomes the testable evidence. Whichever redesign is adopted will be locked as a dated amendment before evaluation. Until then, this quantified boundary stands as the primary limitation of the study, replacing our earlier qualitative admission (caveat 3).

\subsection{The Identifiability Score: what this panel can and cannot decide (amendment 2026-09-27 12:06 IST, locked before computation)}
The H4--H4c arc shows the convergence question failing for two different reasons, and the distinction matters: a claim can be \emph{falsified} (tested and rejected) or \emph{untestable in principle} on a given panel (no observation could have rejected it). We formalize the distinction as a per-candidate diagnostic, computed on the 20-species extended panel by replaying the locked H4c permutation streams (same seed, same candidate order; every observed statistic reproduced its committed value).

Let $\mathcal{S}$ range over the shuffled strata (Deinococcaceae, $n=6$; Bdelloida, $n=3$), and let $k_{\mathcal{S}}(x_g) = \sum_{i \in \mathcal{S}} x_{g,i}$ be the occupancy of stratum $\mathcal{S}$. A stratum is \emph{informative} for $g$ when partially occupied,
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{I}_{\mathcal{S}}(x_g) \;=\; \mathbf{1}\!\left[\, 0 < k_{\mathcal{S}}(x_g) < |\mathcal{S}|\, \right],
\end{equation}
since only then does the within-strata shuffle have any freedom at all. The operative identifiability quantity is the variance of the locked null distribution of the gain statistic, and the smallest lower-tail $p$-value any observation could achieve under it:
\begin{equation}
\sigma^2_{\mathrm{null}}(g) \;=\; \mathrm{Var}_{b}\!\left[ G(\pi_b(x_g);\tau) \right], \qquad
p_{\min}(g) \;=\; \frac{1 + \sum_{b=1}^{B} \mathbf{1}\!\left[ G(\pi_b(x_g);\tau) \le g_{\min} \right]}{1 + B},
\end{equation}
where $g_{\min} = \min_b G(\pi_b(x_g);\tau)$ is the smallest gain count the panel's occupancy admits. The classification is locked:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{class}(g) \;=\; \begin{cases}
\textsc{impossible}, & \sigma_{\mathrm{null}}(g) = 0,\\
\textsc{underpowered}, & \sigma_{\mathrm{null}}(g) > 0 \;\wedge\; p_{\min}(g) \ge 0.05,\\
\textsc{testable}, & p_{\min}(g) < 0.05.
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
Partial occupancy is necessary but not sufficient for identifiability: with three bdelloids, a $k=2$ occupancy permits three arrangements, yet all can yield the same gain count because the stratum subtends one resistant branch; the null variance, not the occupancy count, is the decisive quantity.

\textbf{Panel verdict: no candidate is testable.} Nine candidates are \textsc{impossible} -- the structured null is a constant, so the convergence question cannot be asked of them on this panel at all -- and the single candidate with real null variance is \textsc{underpowered}: even its most extreme achievable configuration yields $p_{\min} = 0.299$, an order of magnitude from significance. This sharpens the H4c statement (``power exists only for partially occupied strata''): partial occupancy is where power \emph{could} live, but this panel's one partially occupied candidate shows it need not live there. The score thereby converts a qualitative limitation into a reusable diagnostic with an explicit remedy: only phylogenetically \emph{dispersed} resistant lineages raise $p_{\min}$ below the decision threshold -- additional proteomes within the same clades change nothing, which is the quantitative content of the identifiability boundary. On the original 15-species panel, all ten candidates were \textsc{impossible} (committed H4: zero-variance nulls throughout). No H4 gate, label, or caveat changes; the score is the framework's self-diagnostic layer, and it is what a reviewer can apply to any future panel before believing a convergence claim from it.

\subsection{Genome-wide permutation FDR of the candidate screen (amendment h8b, 2026-09-27 12:13 IST, locked before computation)}
The empirical-$p$ gate calibrates each orthogroup separately; it does not by itself bound the genome-wide false discovery rate of the twelve-candidate list. We estimate that rate by replaying the entire locked decision rule on every one of the screen's own 1000 label permutations. The screen was re-run from its committed implementation with the full statistic matrix retained, $S \in \mathbb{R}^{52{,}786 \times 1001}$, where $S_{g,0}$ is the observed one-sided Fisher statistic of orthogroup $g$ and $S_{g,b}$ its value under permutation $\pi_b$ (same input, arms, hypergeometric kernel, seed, and permutation count as the H1 gate; a pre-committed sanity lock reproduced all twelve committed hit identities with their exact stored empirical $p$-values - eleven at $13/1001$, one at $17/1001$ - before any FDR quantity was computed). Each permuted labeling is then scored by the identical rule, with its empirical $p$ obtained by rank recursion against the same matrix:
\begin{equation}
N(\pi_b) \;=\; \#\Big\{ g : \underbrace{\frac{1 + \sum_{j \neq b} \mathbf{1}\!\left[ S_{g,j} \le S_{g,b} \right]}{1001} < 0.05}_{\text{rank-recursion empirical } p} \;\wedge\; p^{R}_{g}(b) > p^{S}_{g}(b) \;\wedge\; \sum_{o=1}^{3} \mathrm{origin}_{g,o}(b) \ge 2 \Big\},
\end{equation}
and $\widehat{\mathrm{FDR}} = \tfrac{1}{12 B} \sum_{b=1}^{B} N(\pi_b)$. The result is honestly negative for genome-wide control: permuted labelings yield $N(\pi_b)$ with mean 22.3, median 0, and maximum 2{,}561, giving $\widehat{\mathrm{FDR}} \approx 1.85$ (normal 95\% upper bound 2.66). The null is strongly bimodal - most permuted labelings produce no hits at all, but labelings that preserve phylogenetic correlation structure generate hit sets larger than the observed one. Two qualifications are recorded rather than tuned away: the estimator scores each permuted labeling against the fixed permutation set (exact only in expectation under redrawn permutations), and the mean is inflated by correlation-preserving labelings that the $\ge 2$-origins gate only partially removes. The consequence is stated plainly: the twelve candidates carry the strongest per-orthogroup evidence this panel can produce, but no genome-wide error-rate control is claimed for the list; confirmation routes through the mechanistic prioritization arm and experiment. An earlier approximation of this analysis (raw hypergeometric Fisher on a presence-only matrix) aborted on its own pre-committed sanity lock when it failed to reproduce the committed hits; the abort and its root cause are archived in results/h8\_genomewide\_fdr.json.

\subsection{Baselines: simpler selection rules against the framework (amendment 2026-09-27 12:19 IST, locked before list generation)}
Three simpler selection rules were executed against the committed twelve under a locked arm-blind grading protocol (pool shuffled with a fixed seed before any grading; the H5 v2 tier definitions applied unchanged). \textsc{B0 random}: uniform draws from the tested universe. \textsc{B1 sequence-only}: mmseqs2 search of the panel proteomes against the locked S$^+$ reference families, top twelve by $e$-value. \textsc{B2 domain-only}: per-orthogroup resistant-direction Fisher on a panel-wide hmmscan of the locked 64-family Pfam set, with no specificity and no origins layers. Grading outcome: B0 yields 0/36 Tier-1-or-2 entries; B1 yields 12/12 Tier-1; B2 yields 8/12 Tier-1-or-2; the framework (B3) yields 8/12. Under the locked strict-exceed rule the framework beats random selection, does not beat sequence-only selection, and ties domain-only selection - and both non-wins are structurally informative. B1's perfect Tier-1 score is tautological: it selects by sequence similarity to literature-known families, so all twelve of its calls are the canonical families themselves, including copies in \emph{sensitive} species (a \emph{Thermus} DdrA homolog, \emph{Hypsibius} CAHS/SAHS) - it rediscovers the known and finds nothing new, which is precisely what an enrichment screen must look past. B2 returns a list \emph{identical} to the committed twelve: on the annotatable universe the specificity and origins layers confirm rather than filter, evidence that the framework's added machinery costs no candidates. The comparison thus converts a reviewer question (``would something simpler do?'') into a measured answer: simpler rules either rediscover known families or recover the same list with less audit structure.

\subsection{Sensitivity audits: orthogroup calling and assembly quality (amendments 2026-09-27 12:29 IST, locked before computation)}
Two robustness audits were executed under locked report-only rules. \emph{Orthogroup-calling stability}: re-mapping the committed hits' resistant-arm members across the two alternative clusterings on disk, 11 of 12 hits co-cluster ($\ge 80\%$ of resistant members) in the 13-species mmseqs v2 clustering; the single splitting hit (UJR10666.1, folate-pathway) is flagged here and in Caveats rather than removed. \subsection{Radiation-specific or general stress? A classification power limit (amendment 2026-09-27 12:38 IST, locked before computation; delegated execution, reviewed and committed by the lane)}
To ask whether the committed candidates reflect radiation-specific or general stress adaptation, the H1 gate was re-run under two alternative sensitive-arm compositions: lab-model controls only (S-A) and stress-specialist controls only (S-B), both at $N = 8$. All twelve candidates classify \textsc{control-dependent}: none passes the gate under either composition (empirical $p = 0.075$--$0.081$). The sweep is a power property, not biology: every candidate carries zero sensitive copies under both compositions, making S-A and S-B numerically identical, and at $N = 8$ with $p_s = 0$ the permutation floor is $\binom{5}{p_m}/\binom{8}{p_m}$ - $0.071$ at $p_m = 4$, unreachable by any candidate at the locked $0.05$ gate, while the same $p_m = 4$ profile scores $0.0152$ under the committed $N = 11$ panel. The classification question is therefore \emph{untestable at reduced panel size} (the identifiability theme of Section 4.5 recurring at the level of arm composition); separating radiation-specific from general-stress adaptation needs more independent sensitive lineages, not a re-run. Recorded verbatim; no candidate labels change.

\section{Caveats (honesty section)}
An implementation audit (2026-09-27, correction amendment 00:54 IST) found that the first H1 run encoded a 12-slot panel vector over the 11-species true panel, placing \emph{Thermus aquaticus} in the resistant arm with wrong 6-vs-6 Fisher/MWU constants. The locked gates were re-run on the corrected mapping with unchanged thresholds, seed, and permutation count (PRE-REGISTRATION.md): all 12 Fisher candidate identities and all specificity labels survive unchanged, the Fisher floor becomes $1/\binom{11}{5}$, the complete-separation empirical $p$ becomes 0.013, and the secondary MWU arm shrinks from 53 to 26 hits (the earlier constants had inflated it). No downstream claim (H2 labels, H3 domain confirmation, H4 phylogenetic boundary) used the broken code path. The buggy outputs are archived in results/ for the audit trail.
(1) Tardigrada origin is single-proteome (Milnesium terminally unavailable); the gate's ``$\geq 2$ origins'' was met by Deinococcaceae+Bdelloidea in every passing orthogroup - no Tardigrada-supported convergence is claimed. (2) Empirical $p=0.013$ is a 1000-permutation count estimate ($13/1001$) for complete-separation configurations at the true 5-vs-6 panel; the estimator floor is $1/1001 \approx 10^{-3}$ and the exact-enumeration Fisher floor is $1/\binom{11}{5} \approx 2.2\times10^{-3}$, so 0.013 means roughly a dozen permuted labelings scored as extreme as the observed - it is not a continuous measure. (3) Empirical-p calibration assumes exchangeability under label permutation; family-level phylogenetic structure is only partially randomized away. Section 3.4 quantifies this: structured-presence-permutation tests are degenerate on saturated clades, so convergence beyond relatedness is not claimed for any candidate (terminal identifiability statement). (4) 4 esummary records failed uid resolution and await retry; their Pfam calls stand via hmmscan. (5) Candidates are computational; no experimental validation is claimed. (6) UJR10666.1 splits in the v2 13-species clustering (Section 4.8); flagged, not removed, per the locked rule. (7) Genome-wide permutation FDR of the twelve-candidate list is $\approx 1.85$ (Section 3.6): per-orthogroup calibration only; no multiple-testing control is claimed. (8) The v1 headline peroxiredoxin did not survive the specificity layer (Section 3.1); v1-era claims about AhpC-TSA as radiation-specific are superseded by the v2 labels.
